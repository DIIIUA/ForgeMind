# ForgeMind v0.2.10-alpha

Android 10+ · versionCode 12 · Room 10 · ZIP 5 (reads 1–5).

## Highlights
- **TTS Pause / Resume (`⏸ / ▶`):** Instant pause and resume in the chat speaking bar across XTTS-v2, Microsoft Edge Neural, OpenAI TTS, and Android TTS, preserved cleanly across streaming chunks.
- **Smoother, uninterrupted speech:** Unified Producer–Consumer prefetch pipeline for both regular and streaming TTS (`3`-chunk lookahead for XTTS and Edge, `2`-chunk for OpenAI), larger progressive chunks (`Edge 520/1500`, `XTTS 220`, `OpenAI 900/3800`, `Android 1000`), streaming sentence coalescing, `trimPcm16Silence` for XTTS chunk boundaries, and cleaner markdown-to-speech normalization (no stray spaces before punctuation or invisible Unicode spaces).
- **Voice barge-in & unified auto-speak:** Pressing the microphone in chat or floating overlay (or Stop) immediately silences AI speech and blocks auto-TTS while recording; unified `Off / After reply / Streaming` auto-speak mode across Chat and Voice settings; fixed Android STT append & floating STT hallucination filter.
- **UI declutter & visual consistency:** Cleaned Chat `⋮` menu, merged compaction history into Context & Compaction dialog, dismissible compaction banner, compact Terminal toggle icon in composer, `ForgeColors.SurfaceCard` styling across all 12 newer dialogs, unified Settings → Backups & transfer tab, 3-tier tool approval presets, and direct Settings links to Assistants / Memory / Schedules.
- **Offline Ed25519 `SF1` licensing:** Local signature verification (`kid: sf-2026-11`, public key only) with optional online check and encrypted `.fms` transfer support. No existing functionality is gated yet.
- **Complete 13-language localization:** Extracted 233 hardcoded Russian UI strings from 19 screens/dialogs/services into Android resources and synchronized all 13 locales (`en`, `ru`, `uk`, `es`, `de`, `fr`, `pt`, `tr`, `zh-CN`, `ja`, `ko`, `hi`, `ar`) to 100% parity (`1085 / 1085` translatable keys).
